Geometric TD — Balance Dashboard

Everything is measured in one unit: the laser-equivalent (laser-eq) — the firepower of a single un-upgraded Laser tower (≈23 damage/second). A level demands a certain number of laser-eq to clear; a roster supplies some; their ratio says whether a level is too easy, fair, or too hard. Local balancing tool — nothing here is shown to players.
